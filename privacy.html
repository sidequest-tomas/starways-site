Privacy policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026 · Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy covers the Starways Android app, this website and the beta sign-up. In short: the app does not collect or share any personal data, and nothing leaves your phone. The app has no internet permission and is built to stay that way, so it cannot send anything from your phone.

What the app uses, and why

  • Camera. Starways takes pictures of the night sky to find stars. The images are processed in memory on your phone and are not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Motion and orientation sensors (accelerometer, gyroscope, compass) and the phone's clock. These tell the app which way the camera points and when the picture was taken, which it needs to work out your position.
  • Location (only in Advanced mode). If you turn on Advanced mode and tap Compare GPS, the app reads your phone's satellite position once to show how far the star fix is from GPS. Android asks for your permission first. The position is only shown on screen and is never sent anywhere.
  • Physical activity and notifications (only for Track). When you turn on Track, the app uses the phone's step detector to count your steps, so Android asks for the physical activity permission. While Track is on, the app runs in the foreground and shows a notification so you can see that it is running, which is why it asks to show notifications. Step data is processed on your phone and is never sent anywhere.
  • Text-to-speech (voice instructions). Spoken instructions use the text-to-speech engine already installed on your phone, such as Google's or Samsung's. Starways only gives it fixed instruction text, like "turn the phone" or the accuracy of a fix, and never your position or any personal data. You can turn the voice off in Settings.

All calculations happen on your phone. The app works the same in flight mode.

Data stored on your phone

The app saves things like your settings (including night brightness and whether the voice is on) and its sensor calibration in its own private storage on your phone. This data is not included in Android backups, so it never leaves the phone. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting or third-party tracking code.

Purchases

Starways is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment under the Google Privacy Policy. Google gives developers limited order information, such as an order number, the country and the amount. We use it only for accounting, tax and refunds, and we never get your card details.

Beta sign-up

If you sign up for the beta, the sign-up form on Google Forms asks you to sign in with your Google account and shares your email address with us. We store it in a Google Sheet and use it only to send you a Google Play promo code and the test instructions, and to answer if you reply with feedback. We do not share it or use it for marketing. If you join the tester group on Google Groups, its owner can see your email address. Google handles the form and the group under the Google Privacy Policy. To have your address deleted, reply to the email you received and ask us.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts, and its fonts are served from the same site. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Children

Starways is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently, we will update this page and the effective date before that version is released.
